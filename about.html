About Us

Crafting Confidence,
One Set at a Time

At Nailart MW & Co., we believe that beautiful nails are more than just a trend—they are a form of self-expression. Located in the heart of Birmingham, our studio is designed to be a relaxing escape where you can unwind while we work our magic.

We specialize in premium nail care, from flawless Gel X and BIAB to stunning custom nail art. We are strictly an Acrylic-Free studio, prioritizing the health and integrity of your natural nails. Plus, we back our work with a 7-Day Repair Guarantee for your complete peace of mind.
